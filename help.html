Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of our most common questions.

What accommodation packages are available?

SkyNest offers Premium, Standard and Short-Term accommodation packages to suit different needs.

Is Wi-Fi included in the accommodation?

Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the SkyNest property.

Are the rooms fully furnished?

Yes, all rooms come furnished with essential furniture for comfortable living.

What facilities can residents access?

Residents can access shared kitchens, co-working spaces, lounges and gym facilities.

How can I enquire about accommodation?

You can submit an enquiry through our Contact Us page to request further information.
